# Adopt Me indoor-care evidence

Authority: [intent.md](../intent.md) and BUILD.md milestone 3. Research checked October 20, 2026. These official sources support a candidate task and manual setup; they do not establish a tested interaction sequence, resource pack, supported layout or permission to enable unattended care. No care routine is implemented or enabled by this document.

## Official facts

- Sleep is a pet/baby need fulfilled by using a bed. Source: Uplift support, [Making Bucks](https://upliftgames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/39559905397908-Making-Bucks), updated July 22, 2025.
- Standard beds support babies and pets. Source: official [Furniture Storage feature notes](https://www.playadopt.me/news/furniture-storage-feature-notes), published June 20, 2025. This does not establish identical behavior for every special bed or another player's house.
- Needs are displayed above the pet; selecting a pet opens its needs/interaction interface, with the older menu available through a gameplay setting. Source: official [Pet Needs Revamp](https://www.playadopt.me/news/pet-needs-revamp), published November 4, 2024. Exact current button locations and transitions remain unverified.
- A full-grown pet's friendship progress normally awards an Age-Up Potion after thirty completed tasks; the stated 2x Weekend threshold is fifteen. Source: Uplift support, [What are 2x Weekends?](https://upliftgames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/39562318582548-What-are-2x-Weekends), updated June 2, 2026. A fixed thirty-task reward assumption would miss this event distinction. These pages do not establish the owner's proposed no-daily-cap rule.

An event calendar or elapsed time cannot establish that a multiplier is active, that a need exists or that a reward was received. Task and reward counts must follow visible verified outcomes; no fixed number of clicks proves a potion award. Game updates can invalidate these facts or their presentation.

## Candidate supervised setup

The first candidate remains `care.sleep`, subject to live validation. The user would manually enter their own prescribed home, pre-place a standard bed, equip the intended full-grown pet and establish a repeatable starting view. Furniture acquisition, purchases, trades, gifts and pet disposal remain outside automation.

Before writing a care interaction, capture/calibration and consented fixtures must establish the current need indicator, bed/menu sequence, active-pet identity and concrete completion/progress evidence. Need disappearance alone may mean that the indicator is hidden; it cannot prove completion. The sources above do not specify a reliable completion animation, exact menu sequence, shared-house permissions or task duration within the prototype's thirty-second live limit.

Record device/OS/display, Roblox/game context, pet/setup, pack/SDK/app versions and observed failures during repeated supervised trials. Keep private screenshots local and out of source control; dataset use requires its own selection and consent. Until those gates pass, keep care unavailable, purchases/trades blocked and the existing live cap unchanged.
